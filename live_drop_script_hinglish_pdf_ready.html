Aegis Protocol: One-Call Live-Drop Sales Script (Hinglish)

This script is engineered for sales representatives who close deals on a single call by sending an interactive demo link directly to the prospect's WhatsApp/SMS and walking them through it in real-time.

SECTION 1: THE COLD CALL & PIVOT

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, mera naam [Your Name] hai. Main Metrixmedia mein ek Business Coach hoon, jo ek full-service digital marketing agency hai.

Reason for the call today, [Name]—hum local businesses ko stand out karne mein help karte hain unki online rating 5-star build karke, taaki wo zyada customers attract kar sakein aur apna revenue grow kar sakein.

Maine aaj subah aapka [Google Map / Zomato / Swiggy / Amazon / Flipkart] profile check kiya, aur agar aapke paas chhota sa 60 seconds ka time ho, toh main aapko show karna chahta hoon ki hum kaise aapki online presence ko boost kar sakte hain.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition Teaser

"Okay great. See, humne ek in-house, cutting-edge rating software banaya hai jo 1-star aur 2-star reviews ko detect kar leta hai, isse pehle ki user use Google par post kare. Isse aapki online reputation aapki current rating se lekar 4.9 stars tak grow kar sakti hai.

Sabsay best parts ye hain ki ye completely automatic hai, isko setup karne mein chhota sa 5 minutes lagta hai, aur ye extremely affordable hai—ek Starbucks coffee se bhi sasta."

3. The Live-Drop Pivot

"Kal ya parso ka meeting book karne ke bajaye, mere paas ek chhota sa 60-second interactive demo link hai jo main abhi aapke WhatsApp par send kar sakta hoon, taaki aap ise apne phone par live dekh sakein.

Kya aap abhi apne phone ke paas hain?

Perfect. Main ise aapke number par send kar raha hoon jo [Last 4 digits] par end hota hai. Jaise hi aapko message mile, mujhe batayiyega."

SECTION 2: THE INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGH

Once they click the link and open the demo, guide them step-by-step to build absolute certainty in the product:

"Okay, kya aapka page load ho gaya? Perfect.

Ab, aap ek frustrated customer ki tarah act karein jo ek bad 2-star review chhodna chahta hai. 2 stars par click karke ek chhotisi complaint likhiye... aur submit dabayiye.

Aapne dekha kya hua? Google par jaane ke bajaye, system ne aapko ek private feedback page par route kar diya. Wo review sirf aapke aur unke beech rahega. Public ko kabhi pata nahi chalega.

Ab, back button dabayiye aur ek happy customer ki tarah act karke 5-star review chhodne ki koshish karein. 5 stars par click karke submit kijiye.

Dekha kaise isne aapko instantly Google Maps par redirect kar diya, taaki aap publicly 5-star review de sakein?

Yehi hai Aegis Protocol. Ye ek automatic filter ki tarah kaam karta hai. Unhappy customers ko privately rok leta hai; happy customers ko seedhe Google par bhej deta hai."

SECTION 3: THE DIRECT CLOSE

Once they see it work, go straight for the order:

"Ye itna simple hai. Ye bad reviews ko public hone se rokta hai aur saare acche reviews ko aapke profile par funnel karte hain. Isse aapki rating instantly jump karegi aur competitors ke customers aapki taraf aane lagenge.

Jaisa maine bataya, setup karne mein sirf 5 minutes lagte hain aur ye ek Starbucks coffee se bhi sasta hai—sirf 1,000 rupees a month. Toh kya aaj hi aapke profile ko shield karna sahi rahega?"
