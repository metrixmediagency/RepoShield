1. The "Value-First" Cold outreach Script

Send this script via WhatsApp, IG Direct Message, or Email. Make sure to generate their Live Demo link first and attach it!

Subject: Feedback issue on Google Maps for [Business Name] Hi [Owner Name], I was looking at places to visit in [Area] and noticed [Business Name] has a fantastic customer experience, but a few disgruntled 1-star ratings are dragging down your rating average on Google Maps. This is costing you new customers every day. To help, I designed a custom "Review Gater & Booster" card standee for you. When customers scan it, it automatically sends 4/5-star happy reviews straight to Google, while negative feedback (1-3 stars) is sent privately to your mobile phone so you can fix the issue on the spot before it goes public. I’ve already generated your live demo. You can scan and test it on your phone here: 👉 [Paste Live Demo URL Here] I'd love to activate this for your counter. Normally we charge Rs. 4,999, but I want to give it to you for a one-time setup fee of just Rs. 1,999 so you can experience it. If you'd like to put this up at your counter today, just reply to this message, and I'll send over the print files. Best, [Your Name] MetrixMedia Digital

2. Handling Common Objections

Objection: "Is Google review gating allowed?"

Response: "Our portal simply asks for their initial rating to route customer experience. We are providing a private feedback channel for unsatisfied customers, which every excellent business has. Google encourages businesses to gather reviews, and our system ensures your happiest customers do so on the spot."

Objection: "Can I just print a QR code linking directly to Google Reviews?"

Response: "You can, but that is extremely risky. An angry customer can scan that and post a 1-star review on the spot. Our system gates that, routing angry customers to a private suggestion form, protecting your brand reputation."
