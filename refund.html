Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At METRIX Technologies ("METRIX", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by ONEGRV, we provide B2B in-store tabletop ordering software, digital tabletop assets, and VIP customer data infrastructure for hospitality operators. Our refund and subscription cancellation policies are transparent and straightforward.

1. The 7-Day Risk-Free Trial & Money-Back Guarantee

  • All new monthly subscriptions for the Onyx Tabletop and Sovereign D2C Suite are backed by an initial 7-Day Risk-Free In-Store Trial.
  • If you cancel your subscription within the first 7 days of deployment, your card will not be charged (or any initial subscription payment will be refunded 100%, no questions asked).
  • To cancel during your trial, you can click "Manage Subscription" in your billing receipt or email support at billing@metrixmedia.agency.

2. Monthly Subscription & Cancellation

  • Cancel Anytime: Subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly basis. You can cancel or pause your subscription at any time with one click. There are zero long-term lock-in contracts.
  • Billing Cycle: Upon cancellation, your software service, active guest feedback operations, and dashboard access will remain fully functional until the end of your current paid billing period. You will not be charged again.
  • No Pro-Rata Refunds: Once a monthly renewal payment has been processed after the 7-day guarantee window, we do not issue partial or pro-rated refunds for the remainder of that billing cycle.

3. Digital Delivery & Tabletop Assets

  • All METRIX software access, merchant dashboards, and bespoke vector tabletop print assets (PDF, SVG, 300 DPI high-resolution inserts) are delivered electronically via instant cloud download and email upon account activation. No physical parcels or hardware goods are shipped.
  • Venues can immediately print the digital assets or insert them into their existing tabletop displays, wooden blocks, and bill presenters. We provide unlimited digital asset resizing and branding reconfigurations during active subscriptions at zero additional cost.

4. Payment Processing & Invoicing

  • All international transactions are securely processed by our authorized Merchant of Record and certified payment processing partners.
  • Compliant commercial tax invoices detailing UAE VAT / international tax breakdowns and transaction receipts are automatically issued to your billing email upon every renewal.
