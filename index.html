How does real-time guest feedback help resolve table service issues?

When a guest scans the digital tabletop QR, they can share real-time feedback on their dining experience. If a diner notes dissatisfaction with food temperature or service speed, an instant notification is routed to floor management's WhatsApp in under 3 seconds. This allows your team to visit the table and resolve the issue in person before the guest departs. Guests also have direct, transparent access to public review platforms like Google Maps.

Does the diner need to download an app?

No. METRIX utilizes universal touchless web protocols. When a customer scans the dynamic QR at their table with any smartphone camera, your portal opens in their native browser in under 0.8 seconds. Zero app installs, zero friction.

How does the 7-Day Free Trial work?

If you are thrilled with the captured VIP phone numbers and real-time guest recovery, you continue at our flat subscription rate. If not, cancel with one click with zero questions asked.

Do you take a percentage cut of our direct delivery orders?

Never. Aggregators like Talabat, Deliveroo, and Zomato take 25% to 32% of your revenue. METRIX is pure software infrastructure. You pay a predictable, flat monthly subscription fee, and you keep 100% of your order revenue directly into your bank account.

Who owns the customer database?

You own 100% of the customer data. Every customer name, WhatsApp number, and birthday captured at your tables belongs exclusively to your venue. You can export your full database via CSV with a single click at any time.
