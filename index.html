How does the Review Shield prevent 1-star Google reviews?

When a guest taps the counter standee, they rate their experience. Guests who choose 4 or 5 stars are immediately routed to your public Google Maps listing. Guests who rate 1, 2, or 3 stars are routed to a private concierge feedback gate. The complaint is sent privately to the General Manager's WhatsApp in under 3 seconds, allowing management to resolve the issue before an angry public review is posted.

Does the diner need to download an app?

No. METRIX utilizes native Apple CoreNFC and Android touchless protocols. When a customer taps their smartphone against the Matte Black Hub, your portal opens in their native browser in under 0.8 seconds. Zero app installs, zero friction.

You own 100% of the customer data. Every customer name, WhatsApp number, and birthday captured at your counter belongs exclusively to your venue. You can export your full database via CSV with a single click at any time.
