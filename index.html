Why should I trust MetrixMedia over traditional agencies?

Most agencies hide behind vanity stats like "impressions" or "engagement clicks". We focus strictly on bottom-line outcomes: organic 5-star ratings, search engine rankings, and qualified conversions. Plus, you retain 100% ownership of your accounts and setups.

How does the 7-day refund guarantee work?

If you're not satisfied with the setup, speed, or performance of your active portals within the first 7 days of your billing cycle, we will issue a full refund of your subscription fee, no questions asked.

How does private negative feedback gating protect my brand?

When a customer scans your portal QR code, they choose a rating. 4-5 star ratings link immediately to your public Google Business profile. 1-3 star ratings open a private comment form, which alerts your management instantly to resolve the issue, preventing bad reviews from hitting public listings.

How quickly can we launch our review gating portal?

We deploy standard gating portals and deliver print-ready QR code standee sheets within 24-48 hours of onboarding. Custom domain linkages and API setups are finalized in 3-4 business days.
