Frequently Answered Truths

Everything you need to know about our digital tabletop assets, guest intelligence, and floor service operations.

How does METRIX streamline table service and floor operations?

When seated guests scan the dynamic QR on your table, they can request water, call table service, or ask for the bill with one tap. Floor management receives an instant WhatsApp alert in under 3 seconds specifying the exact table number and request. Guests also get seamless, direct access to leave Google Maps reviews or join your VIP rewards club.

Does the diner need to download an app or register?

No. METRIX utilizes universal touchless web protocols. When a customer scans the dynamic QR at their table with any smartphone camera, your portal opens in their native browser in under 0.8 seconds. Zero app installs, zero friction.

How do floor teams receive table calls?

Service alerts are routed instantly to floor staff or management WhatsApp phones in real time. The notification displays the exact table number and the specific request (Water Refill, Server Call, or Check Request) so staff can respond in seconds without diners ever waving their hands.

How does the 7-Day Free Tabletop Pilot work?

Who owns the customer database?

You own 100% of the customer data. Every customer name, WhatsApp number, and birthday captured at your tables belongs exclusively to your venue. You can export your full database via CSV with a single click at any time.
