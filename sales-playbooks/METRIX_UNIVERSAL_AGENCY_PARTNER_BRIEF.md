## 1. The Strategic Opportunity

### The Attribution Gap in Modern Hospitality Marketing
Creative and performance marketing agencies are under constant pressure from restaurant, cafe, and lounge owners to answer one critical question:

> *"Your social media reels and posts got 50,000 views... but how many paying guests actually walked through my door and spent money at my tables because of it?"*

When agencies cannot prove direct in-store footfall and repeat revenue, their marketing retainers become vulnerable to budget cuts—especially during seasonal transitions and off-peak months.

### The In-Store Operating Layer
**METRIX** bridges the chasm between digital marketing and physical dining tables. We provide marketing agencies with a turnkey, physical and cloud-based **in-store operating layer** deployed directly onto high-dwell dining tables (via compact 100mm × 155mm luxury acrylic and wooden tabletop touchpoints).

Instead of leaving diners as anonymous strangers who pay and vanish, METRIX turns table dwell time into verified customer databases, instant floor service protection, and automated repeat dining loops—**attributed directly to the partner agency.**
