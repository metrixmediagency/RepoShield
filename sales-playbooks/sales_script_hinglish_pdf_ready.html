Aegis Protocol: Two-Call Sales Script & Straight Line Close (Hinglish Version)

This document outlines the strict Two-Call Sales Process for selling Aegis Protocol to businesses.

PHASE 1: THE COLD CALL (Qualify & Book)

The Goal: Hit their pain point immediately, qualify them as a buyer, and book a follow-up meeting. Do not pitch the full product on this call. Keep it under 2 minutes.

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, mera naam [Your Name] hai. Main Metrixmedia mein ek Business Coach hoon, jo ek full-service digital marketing agency hai.

Reason for the call today, [Name]—hum local businesses ko stand out karne mein help karte hain unki online rating 5-star build karke, taaki wo zyada customers attract kar sakein aur apna revenue grow kar sakein.

Maine aaj subah aapka [Google Map / Zomato / Swiggy / Amazon / Flipkart] profile check kiya, aur agar aapke paas chhota sa 60 seconds ka time ho, toh main aapko show karna chahta hoon ki hum kaise aapki online presence ko boost kar sakte hain.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition

(Wait for their response. If they say "yes" or show interest, proceed:)

"Okay great. Humne ek in-house, cutting-edge rating software banaya hai jo 1-star aur 2-star reviews ko detect kar leta hai, isse pehle ki user use Google par post kare. Isse aapki online reputation aapki current rating se lekar 4.9 stars tak grow kar sakti hai.

Sabsay best parts ye hain:
* Ye completely automatic hai, matlab aapko isko manually monitor karne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai.
* Isko setup karna behad easy hai (isme chhota sa 5 minutes lagta hai).
* Aur ye extremely affordable hai—ek Starbucks coffee se bhi sasta.

Hum isko dekhne ke liye ek chhota sa time set karein, usse pehle mere paas aapse poochne ke liye bas kuch quick questions hain takki main check kar sakoon ki kya ye sach mein aapke business ke liye fit hai ya nahi."

3. The Qualification

Question 1: Importance of Online Rating

"Aapke business ke liye online rating kitni important hai?"
(If they say it's not important, they might need educating. If important, proceed.)

Question 2: Rating Management History

"Kya aap abhi, ya past mein kabhi, apni rating manage karne ke liye koi service use kar rahe the?"

Question 3: Agency Partnership

"Kya aap abhi kisi digital marketing agency ke sath kaam kar rahe hain?"

(If yes, follow up with:)
  • "Oh, really? Wo specific kis service ke liye aapki help kar rahe hain?"
  • "Aur unke sath aapka abhi tak ka experience kaisa raha hai—kya aapko wo results mil rahe hain jiske liye aapne pay kiya hai?"

Question 4: The Emotional Pain Point

"Maine notice kiya ki online aapke kuch lower reviews hain. Main aapse poochna chahta hoon—kaisa lagta hai jab aap itni mehnat karte hain, aur phir koi ek random 1 ya 2-star review dekar aapki rating ko niche gira deta hai?"
(Listen carefully. This sets up the pain.)

Question 5: Decision Maker

"By the way, kya main abhi owner ya main decision maker se baat kar raha hoon, ya koi aur partner bhi hain jinse aap in decisions ke liye salah karte hain?"
(If no: "Got it. Toh kisse baat karna sabse sahi rahega, aur unse contact karne ka best time kya hoga?")

4. Booking the Meeting (The Cliffhanger)

"Aapne jo bhi bataya, uske hisab se aap humare banaye hue system ke liye perfect fit hain jise hum 'Aegis Protocol' kehte hain. Ye system bad reviews ko internet par aane se pehle hi literally intercept kar leta hai, aur aapke happy customers ko automatically 5 stars dene ke liye funnel karta hai.

Main jaanta hoon ki aap business chalane mein abhi busy hain. Kal ka kaunsa time best rahega ek quick, 3-minute screen-share ke liye takki main aapko exactly dikha sakoon ki ye kaise kaam karta hai?"

(Lock in the time and end the call).

PHASE 2: THE MEETING (Pitch & Close)

The Goal: Explain the Aegis mechanism clearly, ask for the order, and use the Straight Line Loops to handle objections.

1. The Pitch (The Aegis Protocol)

"Hey [Name], time nikalne ke liye thanks. Jaisa maine promise kiya tha, main ise 3 minutes ka hi rakhunga.

"Aegis Protocol exactly kaise kaam karta hai, main aapko batata hoon: Hum aapke business ko apne high-tech protocol par register karte hain aur ek dynamic, active feedback link ya QR code generate karte hain. Jab aapke customers link par click karenge ya code scan karenge, toh wo seedhe Google Maps par jaane ke bajaye humare private portal par redirect ho jayenge jahan ye protocol chal raha hai.

"Ye system har ek review ko actively check karta hai. Agar koi 1 se 3-star review dene ki koshish karta hai, toh system use intercept kar leta hai aur use private feedback mein convert kar deta hai. Har din, hum aapko in bad reviews aur unke piche ke reasons ki ek list email karenge, takki aap internally un cheezon ko improve kar sakein.

"Lekin jo log 4 ya 5 stars dena chahte hain, unhe humara system instantly Google Maps par redirect kar deta hai, jahan wo publicly aapko wo 5-star rating de sakte hain jise poori duniya dekh sake.

"Ye ek perfect middleman ki tarah kaam karta hai. Agar koi customer frustrated hai, toh humara system unhe Google Maps tak pahunchne se pehle hi catch kar leta hai. Duniya mein kisi ko pata bhi nahi chalega ki koi problem thi. Par agar wo aapke business ke bahut bade fan hain, toh ye unhe seedhe top par funnel kar deta hai.

"Toh literally overnight, aap bad reviews ko public hone se rokte hain, aur saare acche reviews ko apne profile par funnel karte hain. Isse aapki rating turant 5 stars ki taraf jump karegi aur aap competitors ke customers ko apni taraf khinchna shuru kar denge."

2. The Initial Close (Ask for the Order)

"Ye system apna paisa ussi second wasool kar leta hai jab ye kisi ek bad review ko intercept karta hai ya ek naya customer lekar aata hai. Ye bahut affordable hai, aur setup karne mein sirf 5 minutes lagte hain. Toh kya aaj hi setup start karna sahi rahega?"

(Wait for their response. If they say "yes", process the payment. If they give an objection like "I need to think about it" or "Send me an email", enter Loop 1 below).

THE CLOSING SEQUENCE (The 3 Straight Line Loops)

When you get an objection, it's almost always a smokescreen. Follow this exact sequence to knock out their uncertainties, lower their risk, and raise their pain.

LOOP 1: Build Certainty (Product, You, Company)

Use these rebuttals to prove the product works, you are an expert, and the company is legit. Then ask for the order again.

Element 1: Certainty in the Product

Rebuttal 1 (The Emotional "Dirty Cup" Story):

"Main samajh sakta hoon jo aap keh rahe hain, [Name], par main aapse ek sawaal poochna chahta hoon: Kya ye idea aapko samajh aa raha hai? Kya ye idea aapko achha laga?

"Dekhiye, humare Aegis Protocol ki sabse badi khubi ye hai ki ye jitna simple hai, utna hi powerful hai. Maan lijiye aapne humari service nahi li. Toh aapke paas kya bachega? Wahi purana Google review link ya QR code jisse aapke fans toh aapki tareef kar hi sakte hain, par sath hi sath haters bhi wahan pahunch sakte hain. Aur sabse kharab baat pata hai kya hai? Unhe aapke naam, business aur revenue ko nuksaan pahunchane se koi nahi rok sakta. Kya aapko achha lagega agar koi ek chhoti si problem ke liye aapke pure business ko damage kar de? Bilkul nahi, aap jaisa koi bhi businessman ye nahi chahega.

"For example, maan lijiye kisi customer ko ek chhoti si, solve hone wali problem aati hai—jaise ek cafe ke customer ko thoda sa dirty cup mil jata hai. Aap samajhte hain ki ye ek chhotisi mistake hai. Par kya ho agar wo gusse mein aakar aapka purana QR code scan karein ya review link click karke Google par bad review daal dein? Ek baar jab unhone ise publish kar diya, aap use delete nahi kar sakte. Wo chhotisi mistake aapki reputation par ek permanent daag ban jayegi.

"Humara Aegis isi cheez ke liye bana hai. Is exact scenario ke liye, ab wo us purane link ya code ko scan karne ke bajaye, humare hi-tech protocol link ko use karenge ya code ko scan karenge. Is baar wo Google page par nahi bheje jayenge. Instead, they are stopped by our system, and their anger is converted into private feedback that stays between you and them. They never drop a public review on Google. If we do this for all your unhappy customers, don't you think this will save your reputation in the real world?"

Element 2: Certainty in You

Rebuttal 1 (The Straight-Line Reintroduction):

"Dekhiye, main aapki hesitation ko bilkul samajh sakta hoon. Main phone par bas ek voice hoon aur aap nahi jaante main kaun hoon, toh main apna ek baar quick reintroduction de deta hoon.

"Mera naam Sunny Gurav hai, aur main Metrixmedia mein Business Coach hoon. Main ye kaam 2022 se kar raha hoon, aur maine apna kafi time yahi seekhne mein lagaya hai ki aap jaise businesses exactly kaise kaam karte hain. Just for the record, mere paas Marketing aur Business Administration dono mein degrees hain.

"Agar aap wahan baith kar ye soch rahe hain ki main bas aapko koi software bechne ki koshish kar raha hoon—toh aisa nahi hai. Honestly boloon toh, main aapke 1,000 rupees se ameer nahi hone wala. Mera pura kaam aapke business ko analyze karna hai, ye dekhna hai ki aapke jaise businesses kahan se revenue lose kar rahe hain, aur use fix karna hai. Main do cheezon par bilkul stand karta hoon: main apne clients ka time kabhi waste nahi karta, aur main massive results deliver karta hoon.

"Abhi aapke business ka sabse bada leak ye hai ki aapke competitors Google par aapko out-rank kar rahe hain. Main bina kisi doubt ke jaanta hoon ki agar aap mujhe sirf 3 minutes denge ye dikhane ke liye ki Aegis Protocol kaise kaam karta hai, toh aap samajh jayenge ki maine aaj aapko kyun call kiya tha. Sound fair enough?"

Element 3: Certainty in the Company

Rebuttal 1 (The Aerotyne Pitch - Hungry & Elite):

"Rahi baat humari company ki, toh dekhiye, main aapko batata hoon hum kaun hain. Humari company ka naam Metrixmedia hai. Hum ek cutting-edge digital growth firm hain jo local market mein apna presence tezi se badha rahe hain. Ab kyunki hum apna footprint establish kar rahe hain, hum un bade aur slow-moving corporate agencies ki tarah kaam nahi karte jo bas aapka paisa lete hain aur aapko ek file ki tarah file cabinet mein daal dete hain. Hum hungry hain, highly aggressive hain, aur humari agency ko elite, certified marketing specialists ki team back karti hai. Abhi humara goal aapke area ke sirf ek ya do key businesses ko select karke unhe ek badi success story banana hai, jisse hum apne results show kar sakein. Jab aap Metrixmedia ke sath partner bante hain, toh aapko koi automated robot nahi milta; balki aapko humari team ki poori undivided attention aur creative energy milti hai. Hum apne partners ke liye haarna refuse karte hain. Sound fair enough?"

"All I am asking is this: if you don't want to go with the cheapest option, then settle for the half-yearly plan, where you will save the second-most money. Fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 1 CLOSE] -> "Toh in sabhi baaton ke baad, chaliye aaj hi isko setup kar lete hain. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 2: Lower the Action Threshold

If they still object after Loop 1, their Action Threshold is too high. First, emotionally re-pitch the 3 Certainties to get them to a 10, then remove all risk to lower their Action Threshold.

Part 1: Emotional Re-Pitch of the 3 Certainties (Product, You, Company)

This combined pitch uses Rebuttals 2 (The Employee mistake + Student Coach + Partnership Vibe) to rebuild trust and address the objection.

"Dekhiye, thoda piche hat kar ek second ke liye sochte hain ki ye system aapke liye kya karta hai. Aap apne business mein 24 ghante toh nahi reh sakte. Kya ho agar aapke kisi employee ka din kharab ho aur wo kisi customer ko thoda galat attitude de de? Ye ek insani galti hai, par kya ho agar wo customer gusse mein 1-star rate kar de aur 'terrible service' ke baare mein ek kharab review likh de? Ab wo rating aapke business par permanently lag chuki hai. Aegis ke sath, wo customer publicly review daalne ke bajaye, humara system use intercept kar lega. Ye unki complaint ko seedhe aapke private email par bhej dega, takki aap internally use correct kar sakein aur public ko kabhi kuch pata na chale.

"Aur main ye option aapke paas isliye lekar aaya hoon kyunki ek business aur marketing student hone ke naate, maine companies ka function sirf software bechne ke liye study nahi kiya. Main ye isliye karta hoon kyunki mujhe hard-working local business owners ko apne competitor corporate giants ke aage paisa lose karte dekhna bilkul pasand nahi hai, wo bhi sirf ek unfair Google ranking ki wajah se. Ek Business Coach ke roop mein mera ek hi kaam hai ki main dekhoon aapke business se revenue kahan leak ho raha hai aur use block karoon.

"Aur main is par bilkul stand karta hoon kyunki Metrixmedia mein hum koi one-off deal karke gayab nahi hote. Wo kaam un giant aur lazy agencies ka hai. Humara pura business model long-term relationships par khada hai—hum aapke business ke sath agle das-pandra saal tak kaam karna chahte hain. Aur wo hum tabhi kar sakte hain jab hum aapko pehle din se massive results deliver karein. Hum tabhi succeed hote hain jab aap win karte hain. Aap hume bas 1 percent trust dijiye, baki ka 99 percent trust hum deliver karke earn karenge. It's that simple."

Part 2: Lowering the Action Threshold (Worst Case / Best Case Close)

"Sabse worst cheez kya ho sakti hai? Maan lijiye aapne software setup kiya, aur ye sahi se kaam nahi karta ya bad reviews ko intercept nahi karta. Toh kya aap apne saare customers kho denge? Nahi. Kya aap wo 1,000 rupees spend karke road par aa jayenge? Bilkul nahi. Aap jab chahein apni subscription cancel kar sakte hain, aur hum 7-day, no-questions-asked, 100% money-back guarantee offer karte hain.

"Par kya ho agar humara system sach mein kaam kare—jo ki already proven hai? Aapko apni online rating par immediate control mil jayega. Aapki rating tezi se badhna shuru ho jayegi. Wo nayi 5-star rating aapke business par naye customers ko attract karegi, jiska direct matlab hai aapka revenue badh jayega.

"Toh worst-case ye hai ki aap service cancel karte hain, apne saare paise wapas lete hain, aur aapka business waise hi chalta rehta hai jaise chal raha tha. Aur agar ye kaam karta hai—jo ki ye karta hai—toh aapke profits instantly badh jaate hain. Literally aapke paas khone ke liye kuch nahi hai aur paane ke liye sab kuch hai. Ye ekdam risk-free investment ban jata hai. Sound fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 2 CLOSE] -> "Toh aapki taraf se absolutely zero risk ke sath, chaliye isko shuru karte hain. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 3: Raise the Pain Threshold

If they object a 3rd time, they aren't feeling enough pain. Remind them of the daily revenue they are bleeding to competitors, then go for the final close.

Rebuttal 1 (The Unreasonable Customer & Action Close):

"Dekhiye, business ke sabse frustrating part ke baare mein sochiye—ek bilkul hi unreasonable customer. Aap sab kuch perfect karte hain, par wo bas ek discount paane ke liye ya apna din kharab hone ki wajah se ek fake 1-star review daal dete hain. Google use kabhi remove nahi karega, aur wo wahan permanently rehkar aapki poori mehnat ko kharab karta rahega. Ye sach mein bahut unfair hai.

"Aur mere is call par hone ka ek hi reason hai ki ek business aur marketing student hone ke naate, maine companies ka function isliye study nahi kiya ki main honest business owners ko online bully hote hue dekhoon. Maine exactly dekha hai ki kaise aapke competitors aapke customers chura rahe hain, aur mera ek hi goal hai ki main in fake negative hits ko block karoon aur us revenue leak ko plug karoon.

"Metrixmedia mein, hum aapse blind trust karne ko nahi kehte—hum chahte hain ki aap hume humare actions se judge karein. Hume apne business ko shield karne ka bas ek mauka dekar dekhiye, aur main promise karta hoon ki aap life-time ke liye humare partner ban jayenge. It's that simple."

Rebuttal 2 (The Emotional & Financial Close):

"Dekhiye, aapne mujhe thodi der pehle bataya tha ki kafi kharab feel hota hai jab aap itni mehnat karte hain aur koi bad review chhodkar aapko niche khinch leta hai. Aur aap 100% sahi hain—ye sach mein bahut unfair hai. Aap har din is business mein apna dil aur jaan laga dete hain, par abhi aap ek chhoti si gusse wali minority ko ye decide karne de rahe hain ki duniya aapke business ko kaise dekhe. Aur ye sirf self-respect ki baat nahi hai; ye actively aapka paisa loss kar raha hai. Har guzarte din ke sath, potential customers Google par search karte hain, wo bad reviews dekhte hain, aur chupchaap apna paisa aapke competitors ke paas jaakar spend kar dete hain. Aap toh unhe dekh bhi nahi paate. Kya aap sach mein customers ko is tarah lose karna aur apni mehnat par attack hote dekhna afford kar sakte hain, jabki iska solution aapke samne hai?"
[THE FINAL LOOP 3 CLOSE] -> "Chaliye is loss ko abhi rokte hain. Hum ise 5 minutes mein setup kar denge, aur agar aapko ye pasand nahi aaya, toh aap apna pura paisa wapas le sakte hain. Fair enough?"

Quick Tips for Cold Calling

  • Call karne ka best time: Aamtaur par mid-morning (10:00 AM - 11:30 AM) ya early afternoon (1:30 PM - 3:00 PM) jab subah ya lunch ki rush khatam ho chuki ho.
  • Khade hokar aur smile ke sath baat karein: Ye thoda cliché lag sakta hai, par aapki energy phone par saaf pata chalti hai. Owners din bhar logon se deal karte hain, isliye wo high aur positive energy ko achhe se respond karte hain.
  • Apna homework pehle se karein: Call karne se pehle ye pakka kar lein ki aapne unka Google profile check kiya hai, takki aapko pata ho ki unke paas kitne reviews hain aur unki rating kya hai.
