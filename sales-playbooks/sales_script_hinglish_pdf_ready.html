METRIX: Two-Call Sales Script & Straight Line Close (Hinglish)

This document outlines the strict Two-Call Sales Process for introducing the METRIX Guest Intelligence & Dining Operations Platform to hospitality venues.

PHASE 1: THE COLD CALL (Qualify & Book)

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, mera naam [Your Name] hai. Main METRIX se bol raha hoon, jo in-store guest intelligence aur dining operations platform hai.

Call karne ka reason ye hai, [Name]—hum independent restaurants aur cafes ko walk-in diners ka direct WhatsApp database capture karne aur real-time table service feedback resolve karne mein help karte hain.

Maine aaj subah aapka venue check kiya, aur agar aapke paas 60 seconds ka time ho, toh main aapko show karna chahta hoon ki hum luxury digital tabletop assets ke through walk-in customers ko direct repeat diners kaise banate hain.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition

(Wait for their response. If they say "yes", proceed:)

"Great. Aggregators 30% commission lete hain aur customer data bhi nahi dete. Humne ek cutting-edge in-store guest intelligence system banaya hai jo luxury tabletop assets ke through dining tables par deploy hota hai. Diners VIP privileges unlock karte hain, aur agar kisi guest ko food ya service mein koi issue ho, toh floor team ke WhatsApp par 3 seconds mein real-time alert aa jata hai.

Iske sabse acche benefits:
* Zero POS integration—har smartphone par instant load hota hai.
* 5 minutes mein cloud setup aur bespoke 300 DPI vector assets.
* 7-day risk-free in-store trial.

Meeting set karne se pehle, bas do quick questions taaki confirm ho sake ki ye aapke venue ke liye perfect fit hai."

3. The Qualification

Question 1: Direct Ordering & Data Capture

"Abhi aapke walk-in guests ka phone number capture karne ke liye aap kya system use kar rahe hain, taaki third-party apps ko 30% commission na dena pade?"

Question 2: Floor Service Operations

"Jab kisi table par food ya service mein koi issue hota hai, toh floor team ko guest ke nikalne se pehle kaise pata chalta hai?"

4. Booking the Meeting (The Cliffhanger)

"Aapne jo bataya, us hisaab se aapka venue METRIX ke liye 100% ideal fit hai. Ye har dining table se direct WhatsApp database capture karta hai aur floor team ko real-time table alert deta hai.

Aap abhi service mein busy honge. Kal ke liye 3-minute screen-share ka best time kya rahega taaki main aapke phone par live dikha sakoon?"

(Lock in the time and end the call).

PHASE 2: THE MEETING (Pitch & Close)

1. The Pitch (The METRIX Platform)

"Hey [Name], thanks time nikalne ke liye. Main sirf 3 minutes mein explain kar deta hoon.

"METRIX simple tarike se kaam karta hai: Hum aapke venue ke branding ke hisaab se custom portal setup karte hain aur 300 DPI high-resolution vector tabletop print assets provide karte hain jo aapke existing tabletop holders mein 60 seconds mein fit ho jaate hain.

"Diners table par baith kar scan karte hain. Agar unhe food ya service mein koi issue hota hai, toh management ke WhatsApp par 3 seconds ke andar instant alert aa jata hai table number ke sath. Aapki floor team guest ke checkout se pehle table par jakar issue solve kar leti hai.

"Happy diners ko VIP privileges unlock karne aur direct Google Maps par 1-tap review dene ka option milta hai.

"Aapka direct first-party WhatsApp customer database build hota hai, aggregator commission bachta hai, aur weekend floor operations smooth rehte hain."

2. The Initial Close (Ask for the Order)

"Ye platform pehle hi weekend service mein apna poora subscription recover kar leta hai. Flat SaaS pricing hai, zero setup friction hai. Toh kya is weekend ke liye aapka 7-day risk-free trial deploy karein?"

THE CLOSING SEQUENCE (The 3 Straight Line Loops)

LOOP 1: Build Certainty (Product, You, Company)

Element 1: Certainty in the Product (The Operational Service Story)

"Main samajh sakta hoon jo aap keh rahe hain, [Name], par main aapse ek sawaal poochna chahta hoon: Kya ye idea aapko samajh aa raha hai? Kya ye idea aapko achha laga?

"Dekhiye, METRIX platform ki sabse badi khubi ye hai ki ye real-world dining friction ko instantly solve karta hai. Maan lijiye aap humara system use nahi kar rahe. Agar kisi guest ko ek minor service issue aata hai—jaise soup thanda mila ya drink delayed aayi—aur unhone waiter ko kuch nahi bola, toh wo unhappy hokar chale jaate hain. Aur baad mein Google par permanent negative review drop kar dete hain.

"METRIX isi cheez ke liye bana hai. Dining tables par humare tabletop assets ke through diner instant feedback deta hai. 3 seconds ke andar management ke WhatsApp par alert aa jata hai: 'Table 4 notes soup lukewarm.' Floor team bill banne se pehle fresh dish deliver kar deti hai. Ek unhappy guest permanent VIP customer ban jata hai. Kya aapko nahi lagta ki ye real-time floor control har venue ke liye zaroori hai?"

Element 2: Certainty in You (Sunny Gurav Reintroduction)

"Dekhiye, main aapki hesitation ko bilkul samajh sakta hoon. Main phone par bas ek voice hoon aur aap nahi jaante main kaun hoon, toh main apna ek baar quick reintroduction de deta hoon.

"Mera naam Sunny Gurav hai, aur main METRIX mein partner hoon. Maine apna career hospitality growth aur direct response systems ko study karne mein lagaya hai. Mera kaam aapke venue ko analyze karke third-party aggregators ke 30% cut se bachana aur first-party tabletop channels build karna hai.

"Main guaranteed bol sakta hoon ki agar aap sirf ek weekend ke liye METRIX deploy karenge, toh aap khud dekh lenge ki premier venues humare sath kyun kaam karte hain. Sound fair enough?"

Element 3: Certainty in the Company

"Rahi baat company ki, METRIX independent hospitality venues ke liye built-for-purpose guest intelligence platform hai. Hum heavy corporate software ki tarah kaam nahi karte. Hum aapko dedicated support aur bespoke luxury vector assets deliver karte hain.

"Main aapse bas itna keh raha hoon: 7-day completely risk-free trial ke sath apne weekend service mein test karke dekhiye. Fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 1 CLOSE] -> "Toh in sabhi baaton ke baad, chaliye aaj hi isko setup kar lete hain. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 2: Lower the Action Threshold

"Worst case kya ho sakta hai? Maan lijiye aapne 7 din ke liye test kiya aur aapko laga ki ye aapke operations ke liye fit nahi hai. Aapka koi financial loss hoga? Bilkul nahi, 1 click mein cancel kar sakte hain.

"Par agar ye deliver karta hai—jo ki already verified venues mein proven hai—toh aap har hafte sainkdon direct diners ka database build karenge, table issues real-time floor par recover honge, aur repeat visits ayenge zero aggregator commission par.

"Losing side par zero risk hai, winning side par massive upside hai. Fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 2 CLOSE] -> "Toh zero risk ke sath, chaliye aaj trial start karte hain. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 3: Raise the Pain Threshold

"Dekhiye, third-party apps har hafte aapke margins ka 30% le jaati hain aur customer data bhi nahi deti. Har weekend jo bina first-party data capture ke beet-ta hai, wo revenue leak hai.

"Chaliye is weekend se is leak ko plug karte hain. 5 minutes mein setup hoga, vector assets ready milenge, aur agar aap satisfied nahi hain toh zero charge. Fair enough?"
[THE FINAL LOOP 3 CLOSE] -> "Toh chaliye abhi aapka 7-day digital pilot initiate karte hain. Fair enough?"
