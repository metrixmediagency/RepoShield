METRIX: Two-Call Sales Script & Straight Line Close

This document outlines the strict Two-Call Sales Process for introducing the METRIX Guest Intelligence & Dining Operations Platform to hospitality venues.

PHASE 1: THE COLD CALL (Qualify & Book)

The Goal: Address aggregator commission and table churn, qualify the prospect as a buyer, and book a quick demonstration. Keep it under 2 minutes.

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, my name is [Your Name] with METRIX, an in-store guest intelligence platform.

Reason for the call today, [Name]—we help independent hospitality venues capture walk-in diners' WhatsApp numbers directly at dining tables and resolve table service feedback in real-time before guests leave.

I was checking your venue online this morning, and if you have just 60 seconds, I'd like to show you how we deploy luxury digital tabletop assets to turn walk-in diners into direct repeat customers.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition

(Wait for their response. If they say "yes" or show interest, proceed:)

"Okay great. See, aggregator platforms like Talabat or Deliveroo tax venues up to 30% and hide guest data. We deploy bespoke matte black digital tabletop assets at dining tables. Diners tap to unlock VIP privileges, and your floor team gets real-time service alerts on WhatsApp in under 3 seconds if any dish or drink isn't perfect.

The best parts are:
* Zero POS integration needed—it works on any modern smartphone instantly.
* Instant 5-minute cloud setup with print-ready vector tabletop assets delivered electronically.
* Includes a 7-day risk-free in-store trial.

Before we set up a quick time to look at it, I just have a few quick questions to make sure this is actually a good fit for your venue."

3. The Qualification

Question 1: Guest Retention & Direct Orders

"How do you currently capture walk-in diners' contact details to drive repeat direct orders instead of paying third-party aggregators?"

Question 2: Table Service Operations

"When a diner experiences an issue with food or service speed, how does your floor team find out before the guest leaves the venue?"

Question 3: Decision Maker

"By the way, am I speaking with the founder or the operations decision maker, or do you partner with someone else on guest experience systems?"

4. Booking the Meeting (The Cliffhanger)

"Based on everything you just told me, you are a perfect fit for METRIX. It captures walk-in diners' WhatsApp numbers directly at your dining tables, while alerting your floor team instantly to real-time service feedback before diners depart.

I know you're busy running service right now. What would be the best time tomorrow for a quick, 3-minute screen-share so I can show you exactly how it works on your own phone?"

(Lock in the time and end the call).

PHASE 2: THE MEETING (Pitch & Close)

The Goal: Explain the METRIX mechanism clearly, ask for the order, and use the Straight Line Loops to handle objections.

1. The Pitch (The METRIX Platform)

"Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. As promised, I'll keep this to 3 minutes.

"Here is exactly how METRIX works: We configure your venue's custom branded guest portal and deliver bespoke 300 DPI vector tabletop print assets ready to slide into your existing tabletop holders or bill presenters.

"When diners sit down, they tap or scan the dynamic code at their table. If they note any service friction or dish issue, an instant alert is routed to management's WhatsApp in under 3 seconds so your floor team can attend to the table and fix it in person before the guest departs.

"Enthusiastic diners are invited to join your VIP birthday club and share their positive dining experience directly on Google Maps with 1 tap.

"You build a direct first-party WhatsApp customer list, eliminate commission fees to food delivery apps, and maintain flawless floor operations every weekend."

2. The Initial Close (Ask for the Order)

"The system pays for itself in your very first weekend service. It's flat-rate SaaS, zero POS integration needed, and setup takes 5 minutes. Sound fair enough to deploy your 7-day trial this weekend?"

THE CLOSING SEQUENCE (The 3 Straight Line Loops)

LOOP 1: Build Certainty (Product, You, Company)

Element 1: Certainty in the Product (The Operational Service Story)

"I hear what you are saying, [Name], but let me ask you a question: Does the idea make sense to you? Do you like the idea?

"See, the true power of METRIX is how simply it solves real-world dining friction. Let's say you don't use our system. What happens when a guest experiences a minor, correctable issue—like a steak arriving slightly underdone, or a delayed drink order? If they don't say anything to the waiter, they leave unhappy, and three hours later you see a frustrating public complaint online that leaves a permanent mark on your business.

"This is what METRIX is built for. With our bespoke tabletop assets, diners share feedback right at the table. Within 3 seconds, your floor team receives an instant WhatsApp alert: 'Table 4 notes steak underdone.' Your floor manager walks over with a fresh dish before the diner ever asks for the bill. You turn a potential complaint into a loyal VIP guest for life. Don't you think that kind of real-time operational control is invaluable for your venue?"

Element 2: Certainty in You (Sunny Gurav Reintroduction)

"Look, I completely understand the hesitation. I'm just a voice on the phone and you have no idea who I am, so let me just reintroduce myself.

"My name is Sunny Gurav, and I am a partner at METRIX. I have spent my career studying hospitality operations and direct response marketing. My entire job is to analyze your venue, identify where third-party aggregators are eating your margins, and deploy high-dwell tabletop systems that reclaim your customer data.

"I know, without a shadow of a doubt, that if you deploy METRIX for just one weekend service, you will see exactly why independent venue owners rely on us. Sound fair enough?"

Element 3: Certainty in the Company (METRIX - Elite Hospitality Tech)

"As far as our company goes, METRIX is purpose-built as an in-store guest intelligence platform for independent venues. We don't operate like bloated corporate software providers. We give our partner venues full creative muscle, bespoke luxury vector assets, and dedicated support.

"All I am asking is this: test our system completely risk-free during your busiest weekend service with our 7-day trial. Fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 1 CLOSE] -> "So based on all of that, let's just get you set up today. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 2: Lower the Action Threshold

Worst Case / Best Case Close:

"What's the worst thing that can happen? Let's say you test our platform for 7 days, and you decide it doesn't fit your floor operations. Are you out of pocket? No. You can cancel with one click, zero questions asked.

"But what if it works—which it already does across premier venues? You capture hundreds of direct diner WhatsApp contacts, recover guest feedback in real-time on the floor, and drive repeat visits at zero aggregator commission.

"You have literally nothing to lose and everything to gain. It is completely risk-free. Sound fair enough?"
[THE LOOP 2 CLOSE] -> "So with absolutely zero risk on your end, let's deploy your trial today. Sound fair enough?"

LOOP 3: Raise the Pain Threshold

"Look, think about what third-party aggregators do every single week—they take up to 30% of your margins and keep your customer data locked in their database so they can market your competitors to your own diners. Every weekend that goes by without first-party table capture is lost margin and lost guest relationships.

"Let's plug that leak starting this weekend. We'll set it up in 5 minutes, deliver your vector tabletop assets, and if you don't love it, you pay nothing. Fair enough?"
[THE FINAL LOOP 3 CLOSE] -> "Let's deploy your 7-day digital pilot right now. Fair enough?"
