METRIX: One-Call Interactive Walkthrough Sales Script

This script is engineered for sales representatives who close hospitality deals on a single call by sending an interactive simulation link directly to the decision maker's WhatsApp and walking them through the guest experience in real-time.

SECTION 1: THE COLD CALL & PIVOT

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, my name is [Your Name]. I am with METRIX, an in-store guest intelligence and dining operations platform.

Reason for the call today, [Name]—we help independent venues capture their walk-in diners' WhatsApp databases and resolve table service feedback in real-time before guests leave the venue.

I was checking your venue online this morning, and if you have just 60 seconds, I'd like to show you how we deploy luxury digital tabletop assets to turn walk-in diners into direct repeat customers.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition Teaser

"Okay great. See, aggregator platforms take up to 30% commission and hide customer data. We built an in-store guest intelligence system that deploys bespoke matte black tabletop assets at dining tables. Diners tap to unlock VIP privileges, and your floor team gets real-time service alerts on WhatsApp in under 3 seconds if any dish or drink isn't perfect.

The best parts are that it takes zero POS integration, sets up in 5 minutes, and includes a 7-day risk-free in-store trial."

3. The Live-Drop Pivot

"Rather than booking a meeting for tomorrow, I have a 60-second interactive simulation link that I can ping to your WhatsApp right now so you can see it work live on your phone.

Are you near your phone right now?

Perfect. I am sending it to your number ending in [Last 4 digits] right now. Let me know the second you receive the message."

SECTION 2: THE INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGH

Once they click the link and open the demo, guide them step-by-step through the dining experience:

"Okay, do you see the page loaded? Perfect.

Now, imagine a guest at table 4 whose steak was served cold or who needs urgent service. They tap 'Report Issue' or 'Call Waiter' and type a quick note... now hit submit.

Look at what happens instantly: management receives a real-time WhatsApp alert with the table number and exact request within 3 seconds, allowing your floor team to attend to the table and fix the issue before the diner even pays the bill.

Now, look at the VIP Dining Club button right below.

See how diners can join your venue's VIP birthday club, capturing their verified WhatsApp contact for commission-free direct re-orders, while accessing your Google Maps page and menu with 1 tap?

That is the METRIX platform. It gives management real-time operational eyes on every table while building a first-party customer database for direct repeat orders."

SECTION 3: THE DIRECT CLOSE

Once they see the real-time flow in action, close the deal:

"It's that simple. You resolve dining issues on the floor, capture direct WhatsApp databases, and drive repeat visits at zero commission.

We provide bespoke 300 DPI vector tabletop print assets and cloud portal setup with a 7-day risk-free trial. Sound fair enough to deploy your trial this weekend?"
