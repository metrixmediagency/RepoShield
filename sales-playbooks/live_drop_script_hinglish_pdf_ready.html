METRIX: One-Call Interactive Walkthrough Sales Script (Hinglish)

This script is engineered for sales representatives who close hospitality deals on a single call by sending an interactive simulation link directly to the decision maker's WhatsApp and walking them through the guest experience in real-time.

SECTION 1: THE COLD CALL & PIVOT

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, mera naam [Your Name] hai. Main METRIX se bol raha hoon, jo in-store guest intelligence aur dining operations platform hai.

Call karne ka reason ye hai, [Name]—hum independent restaurants aur cafes ko walk-in diners ka direct WhatsApp database capture karne aur real-time table service feedback resolve karne mein help karte hain.

Maine aaj subah aapka venue check kiya, aur agar aapke paas 60 seconds ka time ho, toh main aapko show karna chahta hoon ki hum luxury digital tabletop assets ke through walk-in customers ko direct repeat diners kaise banate hain.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition Teaser

"Great. Aggregators 30% commission lete hain aur customer data bhi nahi dete. Humne ek cutting-edge in-store guest intelligence system banaya hai jo luxury tabletop assets ke through dining tables par deploy hota hai. Diners VIP privileges unlock karte hain, aur agar kisi guest ko food ya service mein koi issue ho, toh floor team ke WhatsApp par 3 seconds mein real-time alert aa jata hai.

Isme zero POS integration chahiye, 5 minutes mein setup hota hai, aur 7-day risk-free trial ke sath aata hai."

3. The Live-Drop Pivot

"Kal ki meeting book karne ke bajaye, mere paas ek 60-second ka interactive demo link hai jo main abhi aapke WhatsApp par ping kar raha hoon taaki aap ise apne phone par live dekh sakein.

Kya aap abhi phone ke paas hain?

Perfect. Main aapke number par send kar raha hoon jo [Last 4 digits] par end hota hai. Jaise hi message mile, mujhe batayein."

SECTION 2: THE INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGH

Once they click the link and open the demo, guide them step-by-step through the dining experience:

"Okay, page load ho gaya? Perfect.

Ab maan lijiye Table 4 par kisi guest ko koi service issue aaya ya instant waiter chahiye. 'Report Issue' ya 'Call Waiter' click karke ek note likhiye aur submit dabayein.

Dekha kya hua? 3 seconds ke andar management ke WhatsApp par instant service alert aa gaya table number ke sath. Aapki floor team guest ke checkout karne se pehle table par jakar issue solve kar sakti hai.

Ab neeche dekhiye VIP Dining Club ka button.

Dekha kaise guests direct VIP club join karke apna WhatsApp number share karte hain direct repeat orders ke liye, sath mein open Google Maps review link aur digital menu access milta hai 1 tap mein?

Yehi METRIX platform hai—har table par real-time guest recovery aur direct WhatsApp customer database capture."

SECTION 3: THE DIRECT CLOSE

Once they see the real-time flow in action, close the deal:

"Ye itna simple hai. Service issues real-time floor par resolve hote hain, direct customer data capture hota hai, aur commission-free repeat visits aate hain.

Hum bespoke 300 DPI print-ready tabletop assets aur custom portal provide karte hain 7-day risk-free trial ke sath. Toh kya is weekend ke liye aapka digital pilot deploy karein?"
