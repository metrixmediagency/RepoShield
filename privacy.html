Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At METRIX Technologies ("METRIX", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by ONEGRV, privacy, data security, and client data sovereignty are core to our architectural philosophy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and secure merchant data and guest loyalty data when using our software, digital tabletop assets, and digital portals.

1. Information We Collect

  • Merchant Account Information: When onboarding, we collect your business name, venue location, manager contact name, business email, and WhatsApp/phone number for real-time guest alert routing.
  • In-Store Guest Engagement Data: When customers interact with an in-store METRIX digital tabletop touchpoint or scan a QR code, they may voluntarily provide their name, phone/WhatsApp number, date of birth (for VIP reward loyalty), and service requests or dining notes.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized interaction logs such as timestamp of scan, browser user-agent, and portal completion status to ensure system reliability and combat automated bot abuse.

2. Financial & Payment Data

  • Merchant of Record: All international payments, subscription billing, and renewals are handled directly by our authorized Merchant of Record and certified payment processing partners.
  • No Stored Card Data: METRIX never stores, sees, or accesses your credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials. All financial transactions are secured under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance through our authorized payment processors and banking infrastructure.

3. Client Data Sovereignty (Zero Data Selling)

  • You Own Your Database: Guest lists, VIP contact information, and feedback records captured at your venues belong exclusively to your business. METRIX does not claim ownership of guest records.
  • No Commercial Data Brokering: We will never sell, rent, monetize, or lease your guest data or business contacts to third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or brokers.
  • Instant Export: You may export your entire customer feedback and VIP database at any time in standard CSV format from your administrative dashboard.

4. Infrastructure & Security Architecture

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between guest mobile devices, our digital portals, and our cloud infrastructure is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS) 256-bit encryption.
  • Database Protection: Backend databases are hosted on enterprise-grade Supabase infrastructure with granular Row Level Security (RLS) policies, isolated schema access, and regular encrypted backups.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Merchant and guest records are retained for the duration of the active subscription to maintain historical reporting and VIP loyalty tracking. You have the right to request full deletion of your business account and all associated guest data by emailing privacy@metrixmedia.agency. Data deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

6. Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding data processing, security compliance, or privacy practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@metrixmedia.agency.
