The 10k to 100k Sales Academy Playbook

🎯 How to Find 50 High-Probability Prospects in 30 Minutes

  1. Open Google Maps and search for a niche + city, e.g., "Dental clinic Indiranagar, Bangalore" or "Cafe Bandra, Mumbai".
  2. Filter for businesses that have a rating between **3.8 and 4.3 stars** with at least 15 reviews.
    • *Why?* They are actively getting customers but suffering from negative reviews pulling down their average. They are desperate for more positive reviews.
  3. Look at their website link: If they have a basic or outdated website, they are an even higher-value target!
  4. Note down their business name, GMB link, and email/social handles (usually listed on their site).
  5. Use this **MetrixMedia Dashboard** to instantly generate a custom portal and QR Standee preview for them. Having a completed mockup ready *before* reaching out increases response rates by 400%.

🤝 Handling Objections & Closing

  • Objection: "Is Google review gating allowed?"
    • Response: "Our portal simply asks for their initial rating to route customer experience. We are providing a private feedback channel for unsatisfied customers, which every excellent business has. Google encourages businesses to actively gather reviews, and our system ensures your happiest customers do so on the spot."
  • Objection: "Can I just print a QR code linking directly to Google Reviews?"
    • Response: "You can, but that is extremely risky. An angry customer can scan that and post a 1-star review on the spot. Our system gates that, routing angry customers to a private suggestion form, protecting your brand reputation."
  • Closing Pitch: Highlight our **7-day money-back guarantee**. Send them the active QR standee digital PDF, let them print it, and explain that if they are not completely satisfied within the first 7 days, they get a 100% refund. This removes 100% of their friction!
