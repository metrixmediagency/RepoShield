Aegis Protocol: One-Call Live-Drop Sales Script

This script is engineered for sales representatives who close deals on a single call by sending an interactive demo link directly to the prospect's WhatsApp/SMS and walking them through it in real-time.

SECTION 1: THE COLD CALL & PIVOT

1. The Introduction & Hook

"Hi, my name is [Your Name]. I am a Business Coach at Metrixmedia, a full-service digital marketing agency.

Reason for the call today, [Name]—we help local businesses stand out by building a 5-star online rating so they can attract more customers and grow their revenue.

I was checking your [Google Map / Zomato / Swiggy / Amazon / Flipkart] profile this morning, and if you have just 60 seconds, I'd like to show you how we can help you boost your online presence.

Got a minute?"

2. The Value Proposition Teaser

"Okay great. See, what we did is we built an in-house, cutting-edge rating software that can detect 1-star and 2-star reviews before a user even posts them. This can help your online reputation grow from your current rating up to 4.9 stars.

The best parts are that it is completely automatic, takes 5 minutes to setup, and is extremely affordable—costing less than a Starbucks coffee."

3. The Live-Drop Pivot

"Rather than booking a meeting for tomorrow, I have a chhota sa 60-second interactive demo link that I can ping to your WhatsApp right now so you can see it work on your own phone.

Are you near your phone right now?

Perfect. I am sending it to your number ending in [Last 4 digits] right now. Let me know the second you receive the message."

SECTION 2: THE INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGH

Once they click the link and open the demo, guide them step-by-step to build absolute certainty in the product:

"Okay, do you see the page loaded? Perfect.

Now, I want you to act like a frustrated customer who wants to leave a bad 2-star review. Click on 2 stars and type a quick complaint... now hit submit.

Do you see what happened? Instead of going to Google, it routed you to a private feedback page. That review stays between you and the customer. The public never sees it.

Now, hit the back button and act like a happy customer leaving a 5-star review. Click 5 stars... hit submit.

See how it instantly redirects you to Google Maps so they can post a public 5-star review?

That is the Aegis Protocol. It acts as an automatic filter. Unhappy customers are stopped privately; happy customers are sent straight to Google."

SECTION 3: THE DIRECT CLOSE

Once they see it work, go straight for the order:

"It's that simple. It stops bad reviews from ever going public, and funnels all the good ones to your profile. This immediately jumps your rating and starts stealing customers back from your competitors.

As I mentioned, setup takes 5 minutes, and it is less than a Starbucks coffee—only 1,000 rupees a month. Sound fair enough to get your profile shielded today?"
